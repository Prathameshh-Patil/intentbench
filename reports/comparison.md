## How these numbers were measured

- **Macro-F1** is over the 150 intents; out-of-scope (OOS) detection is reported separately as
  precision and recall of the `oos` label. Rows without "threshold" take the top class (argmax);
  the served row answers `oos` when the top probability is below the threshold chosen on
  validation by `scripts/choose_threshold.py`.
- **Latency**: one request at a time, median and p95 over 300 test
  queries after a warm-up, on Apple M5 CPU (macOS-26.6.2-arm64-arm-64bit), Python 3.12.14,
  torch 2.14.1, scikit-learn 1.9.1.
  LLM latency is the network round trip when each answer was first fetched.
- **LLM**: LLM few-shot (gemini-3.5-flash-lite), chosen over the other variant by validation macro-F1
  ({'zero': 0.915890442890443, 'few': 0.9344849594849594}). Invalid answers after retry on test:
  0.
- **Cost** uses the provider's paid-tier price per token: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing (gemini-3.5-flash-lite, Standard paid tier; page last updated 2026-10-07, checked 2026-10-08). The local
  models have no per-request fee; the hardware they run on is not priced in.
- **Model size** is the saved model on disk.
